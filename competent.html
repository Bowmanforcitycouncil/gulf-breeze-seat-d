Competent local government

City Hall is not a stage. It is a job.

Show up. Read the packet. Ask the plain question. Vote in the open.

A small city cannot afford confusion. If something is broken, we say so. If we do not know yet, we say that too. Then we find out.

Competent government means the basics work. Permits do not sit in a drawer. Neighbors can find the rule and the reason. Meetings are for decisions, not speeches.

I am Jeffrey Bowman, for Gulf Breeze City Council Seat D. I want a council that treats your time like it matters, because it does.
