Privacy

This is a campaign website for Jeffrey Bowman, candidate for Gulf Breeze City Council Seat D.

This site is static. It does not ask you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through forms on these pages.

If you email info@bowmanforcitycouncil.com, that message is handled like ordinary campaign email so we can respond to you.

Visit counts

We count visits to this site with GoatCounter. It counts which pages are viewed and where visitors came from: the referring website, and campaign tags in links (such as utm_campaign and utm_source) that show which post a visit came from.

GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store anything in your browser. It does not store your IP address or your full browser details, and it does not share data with third parties. It keeps only totals, such as browser name and version, operating system, country, language, and screen width. We do not use any ad tracking on this site.

If we use paid online advertising later, any platform tools those companies provide will follow their own policies. We will not sell your information.
